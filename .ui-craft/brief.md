# ScamShield AI design brief

## Product and audience
Digital safety platform for Malaysian communities and EIS 2026 judges. Guest scanning must be accessible without an account. English and Bahasa Melayu.

## Intent
Professional cybersecurity SaaS. Calm, readable, practical language. The scanner is the primary action; community education supports it. Explicitly distinguish rule indicators from validated model probability.

## Visual direction
Navy #0B1220, blue #2563EB, teal #14B8A6, off-white #F8FAFC. Clean system sans-serif; no remote font dependency. Desktop sidebar, mobile collapsible navigation. Rounded surfaces, strong focus outlines, restrained motion. Dark/light themes.

## Signature
Shield motif and a segmented, explainable risk meter. No invented statistics or claims of guaranteed protection.

## Constraints
React, Vite, TypeScript, Tailwind, Lucide, Recharts. Real FastAPI integration. Local monorepo and Docker deployment are the requested delivery environment; hosted functionality requires an independently deployed Python backend.

## Learned constraints
Follow the supplied full project specification. No fabricated ML scores, private public records, or unlabelled synthetic activity.
